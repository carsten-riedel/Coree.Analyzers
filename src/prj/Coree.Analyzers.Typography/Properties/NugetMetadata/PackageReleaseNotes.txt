1.0.0 (2026-09-19)

 - Initial release.
 - CTYED001 flags en dash (U+2013) and em dash (U+2014).
 - CTYQM001 flags typographic quotation marks (curly quotes and guillemets).
 - CTYAP001 flags typographic apostrophe / closing single quotation mark (U+2019).
 - CTYEL001 flags horizontal ellipsis (U+2026).
 - CTYMN001 flags minus sign (U+2212).
 - ASCII hyphen, quotes, apostrophe, and periods stay silent.
 - Scans C# and matching extra files (Includes minus Excludes plus AdditionalExcludes).
 - Default severity is warning; set warning, error, message, or off per analyzer.
